Frequently Asked Questions

Is low MOQ available for New Zealand small businesses?add
Yes. We specifically cater to small and medium-sized businesses with low MOQ starting from a few hundred pieces per style. NZ businesses ordering uniforms or workwear do not need to commit to large production runs — we work with quantities that make sense for your team size.
Can you make workwear suitable for New Zealand outdoor conditions?add
Yes. We manufacture in polycotton drill, heavy cotton twill, ripstop, and other durable fabrics well suited to outdoor and trade industries. We also produce lightweight options for summer workwear in New Zealand's warmer months.
Do you handle logo embroidery and custom branding on uniforms?add
Yes. All uniforms can include logo embroidery, screen printing, or woven badge applications. We also provide branded neck labels, care labels, and custom hang tags so garments arrive retail or issue ready.
How long does shipping from India to New Zealand take?add
Sea freight from India to New Zealand takes approximately 25–35 days. Air freight is available for urgent orders and takes 5–8 days. We provide tracking and pre-shipment documentation for all export orders.
What is your production capacity and what brands have you worked with?add
Our factory in Noida, Delhi NCR produces 1 lakh+ pieces per month. We have manufactured for leading Indian brands including Reliance Retail, Monte Carlo, Spencer's, V-Mart, and Numero Uno — giving NZ buyers confidence in our quality and scale. Factory visits are welcome by appointment.
