Frequently Asked Questions

How does garment manufacturing in India compare in cost to Portugal?add
Manufacturing in India typically offers 40–60% cost savings compared to local Portuguese or European manufacturing, while maintaining strong quality standards. For European brands looking to improve margins without sacrificing quality, India is one of the most competitive sourcing destinations globally.
Do you manufacture to EU sizing standards?add
Yes. We manufacture to European sizing charts (EU 44 through EU 60 for men's garments) and can accommodate buyer-provided size specifications. We work with EU-standard size sets and grade rules to ensure correct fit across the full size range.
Do you offer OEM and ODM manufacturing for European brands?add
Yes. We offer both OEM (manufacturing to your provided design and tech pack) and ODM (designing and developing garments for your brand). Our in-house design team can create original collections or adapt your references to production-ready specifications.
What is the minimum order quantity for European buyers?add
We offer low MOQ starting from a few hundred pieces per style — making us ideal for Portuguese SMEs, boutique brands, and growing European labels that cannot commit to large production runs. We scale as your volume grows.
How do you handle import documentation for Europe?add
We provide all export documentation required for EU customs clearance — commercial invoice, packing list, Bill of Lading, Certificate of Origin, and any additional documents required by the buyer or customs broker.
