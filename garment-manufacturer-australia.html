ECTA Trade Advantage

Zero Duty on Indian Garments.
Under Australia-India ECTA.

The Australia-India Economic Cooperation and Trade Agreement (ECTA) came into force in 2023. Under ECTA, Indian-origin garments — including shirts, uniforms, and workwear — attract zero or significantly reduced import duty when entering Australia. Australian buyers who previously sourced from China or Bangladesh now have a cost-competitive, duty-free alternative in India.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does India have duty-free trade with Australia for garments?add
Yes. Under the Australia-India Economic Cooperation and Trade Agreement (ECTA), Indian garments attract zero or reduced duty when exported to Australia. This makes sourcing from India significantly more cost-competitive than from China or other origins. We provide the full origin documentation required for ECTA claims at customs.
Do you manufacture Australian standard workwear fabrics?add
Yes. We manufacture in cotton drill, polycotton twill, ripstop, and heavy-duty weaves suitable for Australian workwear conditions. We can match specifications for general industry workwear and can work with buyer-specified fabric standards.
What is your minimum order quantity for Australian buyers?add
We offer low MOQ starting from a few hundred pieces per style, making us accessible to SME workwear brands, hospitality chains, and corporate uniform buyers across Australia. Contact us with your quantity and we will confirm the best pricing tier.
Is your supply chain free from Chinese-origin materials?add
Yes. Our supply chain is India-origin throughout. Fabric is sourced through our parent group Jainam Silk Mills, which has 35 years in Indian textiles. We can provide full origin documentation for compliance and tender requirements.
How do you handle quality assurance for export orders to Australia?add
Every export shipment goes through a 5-stage quality inspection — fabric and trims, cut piece check, in-line, end-of-line, and final finishing. QC reports are shared with the buyer before shipment. We also accommodate third-party inspection if required.
