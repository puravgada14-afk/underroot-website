Morocco & Africa Buyers Ask

Frequently Asked Questions

Can you supply both fabric and finished garments to Morocco?add
Yes. Through our parent group Jainam Silk Mills, we can supply raw fabric directly to Moroccan manufacturers or textile importers. Through Underroot Fashion, we supply finished garments — shirts, uniforms, and custom apparel — ready to sell or distribute in Morocco and North Africa.
What types of fabric does Jainam Silk Mills supply for export to Morocco?add
Jainam Silk Mills exports shirting fabrics including cotton poplin, Oxford, twill, chambray, linen blends, polyester blends, and dobby weaves. Fabric can be supplied in greige, dyed, or printed form depending on buyer requirements. Visit jainamsilkmills.com for details.
Is Indian garment manufacturing cost-competitive for North African importers?add
Yes. India is one of the world's most cost-competitive garment manufacturing origins, offering significant price advantages over European, Turkish, and some Asian suppliers. For Moroccan importers supplying the local market or re-exporting to Europe, Indian-origin garments offer strong margin potential.
What types of garments do you export to Morocco and North Africa?add
We export men's woven shirts, corporate uniforms, knitted T-shirts and polos, school and institutional uniforms, and industrial workwear. We also supply private label garments for Moroccan retailers and fashion brands.
What documentation do you provide for exports to Morocco?add
We provide full export documentation including commercial invoice, packing list, Certificate of Origin, and Bill of Lading. We can coordinate with freight forwarders for port-to-port or door-to-door delivery to Morocco.
