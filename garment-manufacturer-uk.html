Frequently Asked Questions

What quality checks do you carry out on garments for UK buyers?add
Every export shipment goes through a 5-stage quality inspection — fabric and trims, cut piece check, in-line, end-of-line, and final finishing. QC reports are shared with the buyer before shipment. We also accommodate third-party inspection if required.
What is your fabric sourcing background?add
Our fabric is sourced through Jainam Silk Mills, our parent group with over 35 years of expertise in Indian textile manufacturing. This gives us consistent, in-depth quality control from fibre through to finished garment — a key advantage for UK brands who need reliable, repeatable quality.
Can you produce bespoke uniform designs for UK corporate clients?add
Yes. We offer full bespoke uniform design and manufacturing — from concept or tech pack, through sampling, pre-production, and bulk production. We handle custom woven labels, embroidery, branded packaging, and all finishing requirements.
What is the lead time for garment orders from India to the UK?add
Production lead time is typically 30–60 days depending on order size and complexity, followed by approximately 20–25 days for sea freight to the UK or 5–7 days for air freight. We provide daily production updates and pre-shipment QC reports.
Do you supply UK hospitality and hotel uniform brands?add
Yes. We supply uniforms for hotel chains, restaurants, event venues, and hospitality businesses. We can match specific brand colours using Pantone references and supply across a full range of hospitality garments — shirts, tunics, aprons, and outerwear.
