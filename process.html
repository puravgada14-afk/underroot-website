Step-by-Step Process

01

design_services Design & Merchandising

Our experienced design and merchandising team — led by Pearl alumni with 18+ years of experience — creates complete range plans every season. We offer multiple value, fabric, and cost options tailored to your brand positioning.

  • check_circle Trend-driven seasonal collections
  • check_circle Multiple price point options
  • check_circle No-compromise trim & accessory quality
02

inventory_2 Fabric Sourcing & Inspection

Backed by Jainam Silk Mills' 35-year legacy, we source the finest fabrics. Every batch undergoes thorough Fabric & Trims checking before entering production — the first of our 5-point quality system.

Fabric & Trims Checking: Every incoming material is inspected for color, GSM, weave consistency, and defects before approval.
03

precision_manufacturing Cutting & Production

Our state-of-the-art production floor runs CAD marker systems, automatic fusing, and specialized cutting equipment. Production is headed by a veteran with 40+ years of industry experience, ensuring line balancing and zero time loss.

CAD
Marker Systems
Auto
Fusing Machines
04

verified 5-Point Quality Check

Quality is non-negotiable. Every garment passes through our rigorous 5-point inspection system. We also test trims (buttons, zippers) via Pull Test and Fatigue Test for durability.

  • 1 Fabric & Trims Checking
  • 2 Cutting / Cut Piece Checking
  • 3 In-Line Checking
  • 4 End of Line Checking
  • 5 Final Finishing Checking
